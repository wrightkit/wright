Create a Widowmaker headshots-only game mode for the Overwatch Workshop. Write it in OverPy and save it as `mode.opy` in the current directory (a single file).

## Core game mechanics
- Available maps: Necropolis, Château Guillard, Castillo
- 2.5s Grappling Hook cooldown
- A 10 player killstreak grants Infra-Sight
- 10s Infra-Sight duration
- No damage falloff
- No passive health regen or healthpacks
- Heal 25% of the victim's health on kill
- Only headshots do damage

## HUD
- Personal time counter and kills-per-minute pace tracker
- Small HUD: personal stats
- Big HUD: personal stats plus global accuracy and killstreak leaders
- Toggle the stats display and the killfeed separately

## Workshop settings
- Widowmaker HP (default / 175 / 200 / 225)
- Remove damage falloff (enabled by default)
- Kills to win (50 by default, adjustable 1-200)
